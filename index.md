## Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [03.09.2026]*

OffGrid Sudoku is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means in practice.

### What we collect

Nothing.

OffGrid Sudoku does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There is no analytics software, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking of any kind in the app.

### What stays on your device

The app saves the following locally, in private storage that only OffGrid Sudoku can access:

- Your current puzzle and its progress, so you can resume a game
- Game statistics: times, completions, and error counts per difficulty
- Your settings: theme, screen-on preference, and gameplay options

This information never leaves your phone. It is not backed up to any server we control, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it permanently.

### Permissions

OffGrid Sudoku requests one Android permission: vibration, used for feedback when an invalid move is made. It requests no other permissions — no network access for gameplay, no storage access, no location, no contacts.

### Internet use

The app itself does not require an internet connection and works fully offline. Google Play may perform a licence check when the app is first installed; this is handled entirely by Google Play and is outside our control.

### Bug reports

If you choose to submit a bug report, the app opens a Google Form in your browser. This is entirely optional and nothing is sent unless you fill in the form and submit it yourself.

The form is configured not to collect email addresses, so submissions are anonymous unless you choose to include identifying information in your answers. Please do not include personal information in a bug report.

Responses are processed and stored by Google as the form provider, and are used solely to diagnose and fix problems in the app. Google's own privacy policy applies to that processing: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### Purchases

OffGrid Sudoku is a paid app. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive no payment details, no name, and no email address from your purchase — only anonymous sales figures. Google's privacy policy governs the transaction.

### Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

### Your rights under GDPR

Because we collect and process no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. Any data the app creates is stored on your own device and is under your control.

If you have submitted a bug report and would like it deleted, contact us at the address below and we will remove it.

### Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
